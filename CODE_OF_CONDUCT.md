# Code of conduct

Hangar follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and make everyone welcome whatever their background or experience. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not acceptable, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is represented.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, James Garside, through the contact details on his [GitHub profile](https://github.com/jamesagarside). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, close discussions or block people who break this code of conduct, as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
